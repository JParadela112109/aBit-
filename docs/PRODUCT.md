# aBit product vision

## One-liner

College learning, compressed into addictive bites — then you prove it, earn a playful degree, and flex with friends.

## Inspiration

- **Duolingo** — bite size, streaks, frictionless loops  
- **Imprint** — visual, editorial micro-learning  
- **Apple Fitness+ / Apple Music** — award-level motion, type, and restraint  

## Core loop

1. Pick a path (Philosophy · Psych · History · Markets · …) sourced from open university courses  
2. Complete **bites** (30–90 seconds each)  
3. Pass a **checkpoint quiz** to unlock the next lecture cluster  
4. Finish the course → claim a stylized **aBit Degree**  
5. Share progress; climb friend leaderboards; see what they’re studying  

## Content pipeline (Phase 1)

```
Open course sites (Yale OYC, MIT OCW, …)
        ↓ scrape catalog + lecture metadata + overviews
Structured Course JSON (+ license / attribution)
        ↓ bite builder (summarize → cards + quiz stems)
Bite packs for the iOS app
```

We do **not** dump full lecture transcripts into the product as-is. We produce original, attributed micro-lessons derived from openly licensed overviews and outlines, with room later for LLM summarization under the same provenance rules.

## Design north star

Could this win an Apple Design Award?

- Brand-first moments, not dashboard clutter  
- One job per screen  
- Expressive type, atmospheric color, intentional motion (2–3 signature animations)  
- Zero generic “AI purple” chrome  

## Fake degrees (on purpose)

Credentials are playful completion badges — never claimed as accredited university degrees. Copy and legal must stay crystal clear.
