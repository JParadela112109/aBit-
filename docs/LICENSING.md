# Licensing & provenance

## Open Yale Courses

Most OYC lectures and materials: **Creative Commons BY-NC-SA 3.0**.

Required when reusing:

- Attribution to the faculty member + Yale / Open Yale Courses  
- Non-commercial use (unless you obtain separate permission)  
- ShareAlike for derivatives  

Example attribution format from Yale:

> Shelly Kagan, Death (Yale University: Open Yale Courses), http://oyc.yale.edu (Accessed DATE). License: Creative Commons BY-NC-SA

Third-party material inside lectures may be excluded from the CC license — check each lecture’s credits.

## aBit policy

1. Every scraped record stores `license`, `attribution`, `source_url`, `accessed_at`.  
2. Bite cards are **transformative summaries**, not verbatim dumps of transcripts.  
3. Before any commercial launch, legal must clear NC sources or replace them with permissively licensed / original content.  
4. UI never implies Yale endorsement or an official Yale credential.
